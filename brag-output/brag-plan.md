# Brag Plan: Tochno

## What is this app?
Tochno is an AI document verification platform for accountants that reads invoices, acts, statements, and passports, verifies every number deterministically with code (checksums, IBAN fraud, sums, duplicates), and leaves humans only with final confirmation.

## The angle
"LLMs read well, but count poorly." Tochno pairs AI extraction with deterministic code validation — catching the critical errors and fraud that humans and raw AI miss, such as swapped IBANs, fake EDRPOU checksums, and floating VAT numbers.

## Hook (first 0–3s)
Bold editorial statement on warm paper:
**"Мовні моделі добре читають. Але жахливо рахують."**
A live invoice card slides in with glowing scan markers.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Scene 2 (3–8s):** The AI Document Reader in action. An invoice is scanned in real-time. EDRPOU is validated (`32855961 ✓`), line sums sum up (`1 200,50 ₴ ✓`), duplicate check passes (`не знайдено ✓`).
- **Scene 3 (8–14s):** The Caught Fraud / Anomaly Highlight. Red alarm badge pops: `⚠️ ПІДМІНА IBAN — Новий рахунок у старого постачальника`. The exact mismatch is highlighted before it reaches accounting.
- **Scene 4 (14–18s):** The Resolution & CTA: "Один клік: Затвердити і далі". Clean stamp `[ ЗАТВЕРДЖЕНО ]` hits with satisfying sound, revealing **Tochno — Документи, перевірені до останньої цифри**.

## Outro / punchline
"Помилка не потрапляє в облік непоміченою."
Logo: **Tochno** · 30 документів на місяць безкоштовно.

## User flow worth showing
1. Document ingest (PDF/Scan) →
2. Automated multi-point deterministic verification (EDRPOU + Sums + IBAN) →
3. Instant fraud/error flag highlight →
4. One-click approval stamp ("Затвердити і далі").
